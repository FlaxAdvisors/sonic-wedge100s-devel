there is a 'sys' blue blinking LED 'o'
and an 'act' green LED 'o' on the top left of the front panel

each SFP slot/port has two related LEDs '^ ^' or 'v v'
the port LEDs are between the top and bottom sfp openings
two port LEDs on the left: shapped up '^' | down 'v' arrows (in that order) ; and two LEDs on the right up '^' | down 'v'shapped arrows

there are two additional LEDs are on the far right of the front of the switch, labeled top and bot,
and there is an LED selector button for some reasons. pressing it swaps the in/active LEDs:
one is on, the other is off - push the button and that state flips.
